Why Pearl Studio

A small, focused tool built for one job: getting a good-looking Instagram asset out the door fast.

Two export formats

Download a static PNG, or an animated MP4 with a built-in fade / Ken Burns motion — same template, both outputs.

Correct sizes every time

Templates are pre-sized for Instagram's feed post, story, and reel cover dimensions so nothing gets cropped on upload.

No account, no upload

Everything — including video encoding — runs client-side in your browser. Your images and text never leave your device.
